    How the club works

    Choose Hold’em, Omaha, Teen Patti, Five-card Draw or Blackjack tournament. Open Table rules before joining a game. Poker side pots settle automatically. Chips have no monetary value.

    When two or more players have chips, the next hand starts after a ten-second countdown. Turns last 45 seconds. Zero-stack players sit out until they leave and rejoin with chips. Disconnected players auto-check or fold. Inactive seats cash out after five minutes once the hand ends.

    Back takes you to the lobby without leaving your seat. Use Return stack & leave between hands to switch tables.
